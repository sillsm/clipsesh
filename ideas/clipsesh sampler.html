KEYS & WORKFLOW

Ableton-style computer MIDI

A W S E D F T G Y H U J K O L P ; play a chromatic keyboard.

Z/X move the octave down/up. C/V lower/raise velocity. M toggles computer-key input.

Waveform

Drag to select a region. Shift-drag or wheel to pan. Ctrl/Cmd-wheel zooms around the pointer. Double-click adds a slice marker. Shift-click a marker removes it. Drag a marker to reposition it.

Tempo and pitch

The app uses tape-style playback: changing Source BPM → Target BPM changes speed and pitch together. PITCH and FINE TUNE add semitone/cents offsets on top.

MIDI from a DAW

Route Ableton to an operating-system virtual MIDI port, then choose that port after enabling Web MIDI. Incoming notes trigger slices starting at ROOT MIDI.
