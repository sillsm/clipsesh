KEYS & WORKFLOW

Ableton-style computer MIDI

A W S E D F T G Y H U J K O L P ; play a chromatic keyboard.

Z/X move the octave down/up. C/V lower/raise velocity. M toggles computer-key input.

Waveform

Drag to select a region. Shift-drag or wheel to pan. Ctrl/Cmd-wheel zooms around the pointer. Double-click adds a slice marker. Shift-click a marker removes it. Drag a marker to reposition it.

Tempo and pitch

The app uses tape-style playback: a locked source measure is globally stretched or shrunk to PROJECT BPM, so its beat cuts stay on the measure grid when tempo changes. PITCH and FINE TUNE add semitone/cents offsets on top.

MIDI from a DAW

Route Ableton to an operating-system virtual MIDI port, then choose that port after enabling Web MIDI. Incoming notes trigger slices starting at ROOT MIDI.

Test bench

Press T in the window corner. RUN loads external audio dependencies, imports a single test JSON, and compares the rendered loop to its MD5-addressed reference. DESIGN exports the session recipe and dependency hashes as JSON.

Sessions and FX

SAVE SESSION creates one JSON recipe containing dependency MD5s, loop lock, cuts, controls, FX, and sequencer MIDI. LOAD AUDIO FILES supplies the external audio bytes. Click the sample filename to reveal the FX chain.
